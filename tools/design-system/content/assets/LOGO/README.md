# LOGO

The Mix Nova brand board, added on the page: the full mark and its meaning, the wordmark, three mock-ups and the palette. One PNG, 2.1 MB, about 1240 px square, a multi-colour raster, so it is placed with `<img>` and never recoloured.

- The mark: the initials M and N in the violet ramp, a concrete mixer inside the M (the RMC industry), a circular sweep (mixing, a continuous process), a building skyline in the N (construction, strength) and a four-point nova star (innovation). Under it the wordmark "MIX NOVA", a rule, "RMC SOFTWARE" and the tagline "SMART MIX. STRONGER FUTURE." in tracked caps.
- The palette on the board is the token ramp: `mn-purple-600` #6c2bd9, `mn-purple-500` #8a4fff, `mn-purple-300` #b78cff, `mn-navy` #1e1e2e, `mn-grey` #8e8e9a.
- This is the board, not a production file. The production lockups under `assets/Logos/` were cut from it: the horizontal lockup (`mix-nova-logo`), the stacked lockup with the tagline (`mix-nova-lockup`) and the emblem alone (`mix-nova-mark`), each with an on-dark version. Vector exports with the same names replace them automatically when the design source is available.
- On a dark or violet ground use the light-metal version shown on the board's left panel; keep the mark's clear space at least the height of its star.
