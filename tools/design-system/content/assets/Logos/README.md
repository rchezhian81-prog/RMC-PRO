# Logos

The production lockups copied exactly from `apps/web/public/brand/`, cut from the approved brand board (see `assets/LOGO/`). All are transparent PNGs at two to four times the largest size the app renders, multi-ink, so they are placed with `<img>` and never recoloured.

- `mix-nova-logo.png` — horizontal lockup for light surfaces: the emblem (M and N carrying the mixer, the ring, the skyline and the star) beside "MIX NOVA" and the ruled line "RMC SOFTWARE", with "MIX" in `mn-navy`.
- `mix-nova-logo-white.png` — the same lockup for the violet gradient, the login hero, the v2 rail and the dark theme: the navy ink turned white, the violet and silver unchanged.
- `mix-nova-lockup.png` / `mix-nova-lockup-white.png` — the stacked lockup with the tagline "SMART MIX. STRONGER FUTURE.", used only where the tagline is wanted (the login card header).
- `mix-nova-mark.png` — the emblem alone; the browser-tab icon is made from it.

Heights in use: 34px (sidebar), 40px (headers), 52px (login hero); the stacked lockup 168px on the login card. Keep clear space of at least the star's height on every side. Do not add a second tagline beside the horizontal lockup; it is complete with "RMC SOFTWARE". When the design source exports SVGs, the same names with `.svg` take precedence automatically.
