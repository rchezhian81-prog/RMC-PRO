# Logos

Two SVG lockups copied exactly from `apps/web/public/brand/`: a rounded-square mark (rx 11 on 40, `mn-gradient` fill) holding "MN", the wordmark "MIX NOVA" and the line "RMC SOFTWARE". Both are 240 × 52 and multi-ink, so they are placed with `<img>` and never recoloured. They are a simplified typographic stand-in, not the real Mix Nova mark: the real one (M and N with the mixer, ring, skyline and star) is on the brand board under `assets/LOGO/` and has not yet been exported as a standalone file.

- `mix-nova-logo.svg` — for light surfaces: "MIX" in `mn-navy`, "NOVA" in `mn-purple-600`, the small line in `mn-grey`.
- `mix-nova-logo-white.svg` — for the violet gradient, the login hero and the v2 rail: white ink with "NOVA" in `mn-purple-100`, the mark a 16% white square.

Heights in use: 26px (sidebar), 32px (headers), 42px (login). Keep clear space of at least the mark's height on every side. Do not add the tagline beside it; the lockup is complete.
